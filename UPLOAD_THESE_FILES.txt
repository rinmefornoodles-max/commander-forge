THE COMMANDER FORGE 5.10

Upload these four website files to the TOP LEVEL of your GitHub repository:

- index.html
- commander-forge-5.10.0.js
- commander-forge-5.10.0.css
- sw.js

Allow index.html and sw.js to replace the existing versions.
Commit to main and wait for GitHub Pages to deploy.

Changes:
- Player 2's battlefield lanes are mirrored:
  lands are above, nearer Player 2's hand;
  other permanents are below, facing the center.
- Player 1 keeps other permanents above lands.
- Dedicated charge and page counter controls.
- Cards mentioning a named counter automatically show that counter's +/- tracker.
- Charge counters display as a lightning badge.
- Page counters display as a book badge.
- Tome of Legends is tracked with page counters, matching its Oracle text.
