INTERACTIVE TUTORIAL
- “New to Magic?” on the title screen now opens an actual playable tutorial instead of a reading-only lesson list.
- The learner controls one deck; the opponent is scripted and plays its own cards visibly.
- Opponent actions animate through draw, land play, tapping mana, casting, attacking, and blocking moments.
- Several curated opening-hand variants are chosen randomly so repeat runs do not show exactly the same hand.
- The tutorial waits for the learner to perform the requested action before advancing.
- Interactive basics teach: first-turn draw rule, land plays, mana, casting, summoning sickness, attacking, blocking, combat damage, instants, command zone, removal, and commander tax.
- The basics automatically lead into a short guided practice game.
- Guided practice begins from a fast-forwarded Commander board so the complete experience stays short.
- The learner must read the battlefield, target a flying threat, avoid a bad attack, block, use a combat trick, cast the commander, choose multiple attackers, understand summoning sickness, and finish with a flying lethal attack.
- The previous 14-section reading tutorial remains available as “Rules reference.”
- Tutorial progress is isolated from normal saved Commander Forge games and cannot overwrite a user’s table.

The mobile precon scroll preservation and title-screen feedback form from 5.27 remain included.
