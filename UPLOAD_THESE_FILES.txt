TUTORIAL CHANGES
- Tutorial cards now render real Magic card images through Scryfall, with a text fallback if an image cannot load.
- The interactive basics course now begins with a real mulligan decision before turn 1.
- The bad opening hand varies: too few lands, too many lands, or unusable colors.
- Choosing Keep on the intentionally bad hand explains why the keep is risky and lets the learner reconsider.
- Mulliganing gives a curated but variable playable hand and explains what makes it healthier.
- A mulligan-rules explanation covers the London mulligan and the multiplayer free first mulligan.
- The tutorial table now mirrors the normal Commander Forge playmat: opponent hand/stats are at the far top, opponent permanents face the center, your permanents face the center, and your hand/stats are at the bottom.
- The guided game keeps the same short scripted opponent and visible card-play/tap animations.
