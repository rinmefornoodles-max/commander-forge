THE COMMANDER FORGE 5.23

Upload these four files to the TOP LEVEL of GitHub:

- index.html
- commander-forge-5.23.0.js
- commander-forge-5.23.0.css
- sw.js

Changes:
- Public reveals now also appear to the revealing player.
- Publicly revealed library cards are visible and inspectable in both players' library drawers.
- Card Actions reads common Oracle wording for Reveal top N and Reveal until effects.
- Reveal-until effects can reveal one additional card at a time.
- Cards moved from a revealed library selection into hand remain publicly known.
- Enchantments and noncreature tokens use the resource lane opposite the lands.
- Identical basic lands, tokens, and safe duplicate enchantments visually stack.
- Fresh lands and tokens remain beside the existing stack until the turn ends.
- Token dies and Remove token actions work again.
- Custom asymmetric P/T counters such as +1/+0, +0/+1, or -2/+3.
- Derived power/toughness and coach evaluation use custom P/T counters.
- On mobile, Player 1's battlefield is above Player 1's status area, mirroring Player 2.

Both online players must use version 5.23.0.
