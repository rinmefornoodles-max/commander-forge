THE COMMANDER FORGE 5.9 — RULES KNOWLEDGE COACH

Upload these four website files to the TOP LEVEL of your GitHub repository:

- index.html
- commander-forge-5.9.0.js
- commander-forge-5.9.0.css
- sw.js

Allow index.html and sw.js to replace the existing versions.
The versioned JS and CSS files may remain beside older versions.

After committing to main:
1. Wait for the GitHub Pages action to turn green.
2. Open the site in an Incognito/Private tab first.
3. Hard-refresh or clear stored site data if the old version remains.

Major changes:
- Shared card-rules model for the playmat and coach.
- Broader keyword, targeting, combat, trigger, static-effect, and activated-ability understanding.
- Derived power/toughness from counters, Equipment, Auras, and anthem effects.
- Exact executable mana abilities, colors, restrictions, activation costs, life costs, sacrifice costs, and discard costs.
- Summoning-sick mana creatures are unavailable unless they have haste.
- Commander-identity lands produce only legal colors.
- Legal extra land plays, flash grants, casting restrictions, and group attack restrictions.
- Tactical search uses the same legality, payment, targeting, and combat functions as the table.
