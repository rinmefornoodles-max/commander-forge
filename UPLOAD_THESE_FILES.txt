LAND-PLAY RULE

- A normal land play is limited to one during your turn.
- Cards that say you may play additional lands increase the displayed allowance automatically.
- Each player status panel shows “Land plays this turn: used/available.”
- The normal Battlefield action uses and enforces a land play.
- A second normal land is blocked even in Free Table mode while land-play enforcement is enabled.
- Lands put onto the battlefield by a spell or ability use the separate:
  “Put by card effect” or “Put tapped by card effect” action.
- Putting a land onto the battlefield by an effect does not consume the normal land play.
- Settings contains an “Enforce normal land-play limit” option.

MOBILE REPAIR

- Uses dynamic mobile viewport height instead of relying only on 100vh.
- Respects top and bottom phone safe areas.
- The title screen starts at the visible top instead of being vertically clipped.
- Setup, Settings, How to Play, mulligan, token, Scry, and other modals fit inside the usable screen and scroll internally.
- Header buttons and phases can scroll horizontally instead of extending beyond the screen.
- Sidebars, deck fields, invite controls, resource lanes, and card rows stay inside the mobile width.
- The right-side inspector becomes a larger scrollable bottom sheet.
- Zone drawers stay between the top safe area and bottom navigation.
- Toast messages appear above the mobile bottom bar.
- Very narrow screens use fewer columns for controls to prevent text and buttons from being cut off.
