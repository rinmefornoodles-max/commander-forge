THE COMMANDER FORGE 5.11 — BASIC LAND STACKS

Upload these four website files to the TOP LEVEL of your GitHub repository:

- index.html
- commander-forge-5.11.0.js
- commander-forge-5.11.0.css
- sw.js

Allow index.html and sw.js to replace the existing versions.
Commit to main and wait for GitHub Pages to deploy.

Changes:
- Identical basic lands automatically display as one visual stack.
- The stack shows a visible quantity badge such as ×4.
- Tapped and untapped copies form separate stacks so available mana stays clear.
- Clicking or dragging a stack operates on one real card at a time.
- Tapping a land moves one copy from the untapped stack to the tapped stack.
- Nonbasic lands always remain separate.
- Basic lands with counters, notes, attachments, face-down state, or other unique state remain separate.
