How to use The Commander Forge

  1. Choose Solo Practice for local testing or Online Multiplayer for invite-code play.
  2. Paste a decklist or search for an official precon, then validate and choose your commander.
  3. Use drag-and-drop, quick actions, and zone drawers to play the game on-screen.
  4. Open Coach when you want tactical advice based on the visible board and your private information.
