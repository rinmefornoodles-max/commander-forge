# Commander Forge 5.34.5

- Mutate controls moved directly under the selected card name, before Quick Actions.
- A Mutate card in hand/command always visibly shows Mutate, including a disabled no-target button with the reason.
- A Mutate creature already on the battlefield explicitly explains that its own Mutate cost cannot be activated there.
- If another Mutate card in the same owner's hand/command can legally target the selected battlefield creature, buttons are shown to mutate that card onto the selected creature.
- Full Mutate rules remain visible next to the prominent action area.
